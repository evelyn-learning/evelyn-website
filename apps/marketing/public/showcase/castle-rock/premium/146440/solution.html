  1. ▼

    1. Read what the curve shows

    The vertical axis is population size N, the horizontal axis is time. The slope of the curve is the growth rate: steep means fast growth, flat means no net growth, falling means the population is shrinking.

    The question lists the phases in a scrambled order with labels 1 to 4. The task is to put those labels in the order the phases happen.

  2. ▼

    2. Lag phase comes first (label 4)

    A few individuals enter a new environment. They are adjusting, maturing and beginning to reproduce, so births are few and the curve rises only gently.

    Why is growth slow if resources are plentiful?

    Growth depends on how many breeders there are, not only on food. With few individuals, even fast breeding adds few new ones. Many organisms also need time to acclimatise or reach reproductive age before numbers can climb.

  3. ▼

    3. Log phase next (label 1)

    Now there are enough individuals to breed quickly and resources are still plentiful. Births far exceed deaths and each generation adds more than the last. This is the steepest part of the curve, the population growing close to its biotic potential (the fastest a population can grow with nothing holding it back).

  4. ▼

    4. Plateau phase (label 3)

    Food, space, waste and predators begin to limit the population. This environmental resistance (everything in the environment that slows growth: limited food and space, waste, disease, predators) brings births and deaths into balance, so the curve flattens at the carrying capacity (the largest population the environment can support over time, written K).

    Check yourself

    In which phase is the net growth rate about zero?

  5. ▼

    5. Declining phase last (label 2)

    If resources are used up faster than they are replaced, or wastes accumulate, deaths overtake births and the population falls. In a closed system such as a culture flask this is the usual ending.

    Why does the item include a declining phase at all?

    Many textbook sigmoid curves stop at the plateau. In a closed environment the plateau cannot last, because nothing replaces the resources. The item includes a declining phase, which is what happens when resources run out or wastes build up, as in a closed culture, so the decline belongs at the end.

One common slip

"Lag" and "log" look alike. Lag means delay: the slow start. Log is short for logarithmic: the rapid, exponential climb, named because it plots as a straight line on a logarithmic scale. Read the two words carefully before recording the digits.

Show the answer

Doubling at a steady rate is the log phase. As the sugar runs low and alcohol (a waste) builds up, growth will slow into a plateau, and in the flask, where nothing replaces the sugar, the population will then decline.

The idea to remember

Read a growth curve by its slope: gentle (lag), steep (log), flat (plateau at K), falling (declining). The order never changes; only the labels in the question are scrambled.
