Mathematics 30-1 · Item 301823 · Multiple choice · Permutations with repeated letters

Answer: D — 6!3! 2! = 60 arrangements of RED DEER begin with R.

Press play. The tiles move as the tutor speaks.

  1. ▼

    1. Place the R first

    RED DEER has seven letters: R, E, D, D, E, E, R. Every arrangement we want begins with R, so put an R tile in the first position before counting anything.

    The two R tiles are the same letter, so it does not matter which one goes first; that choice does not multiply the count.

    Why not multiply by 2 for the choice of which R goes first?

    Because the two R tiles are identical. Putting the first R in front and arranging the rest gives exactly the same list of words as putting the second R in front. Multiplying by 2 would count every word twice.

  2. ▼

    2. Count what is left

    Six tiles remain, R, E, E, E, D, D, to fill positions 2 to 7. If they were six different tiles there would be 6! = 720 arrangements.

    Need a reminder of what a factorial (the product of a whole number and every whole number below it, down to 1) is?

    n! means n × (n − 1) × … × 2 × 1, the number of ways to arrange n different things in a row. 6! = 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 720, 3! = 6, 2! = 2, and 1! = 1.

  3. ▼

    3. Divide out the identical letters

    Swapping identical tiles does not make a new word. The three E tiles can be ordered among themselves in 3! = 6 ways and the two D tiles in 2! = 2 ways, so 6! has counted each distinct word 6 × 2 = 12 times. The single R left over does not repeat, so nothing is divided for it.

    Arrangements beginning with R: 6!3! 2!

    Why divide by 3! rather than subtract 3?

    Each word was counted once for every way of ordering the three E tiles in their three places: 3 × 2 × 1 = 6 orderings. Over-counting by a factor of 6 is undone by dividing by 6, not by subtracting.

    4. Evaluate and choose

    6!3! 2! = 7206 × 2
    6! = 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 720; 3! = 6; 2! = 2.
    = 72012
    Multiply the two factorials in the denominator: 6 × 2 = 12.
    = 60
    720 ÷ 12 = 60.

Sanity check

All arrangements of RED DEER, with no restriction: 7!2! 3! 2! = 504024 = 210. Two of the seven tiles are R, so two sevenths of those arrangements begin with R: 210 × 27 = 60. Same answer by a different route.

One common slip

Dividing by 2! for the R tiles as well, after one R has already been placed. Only one R remains among the six tiles, and a single tile has nothing to swap with. Divide only for letters that still repeat among the tiles that are left: the three E and the two D.

Explore: begin with a different letter

Before you tap: will more arrangements begin with E or with R?

Begin with:

R: 60, E: 90, D: 60. Together 60 + 90 + 60 = 210, which is every arrangement of RED DEER, because each one begins with one of the three letters.

Try one more

How many arrangements of the letters of BANANA end with N?

Show the worked answer

Fix an N in the last position. Five tiles remain: B, A, A, A, N. Only the A repeats, three times, so the count is 5!3! = 1206 = 20.

The idea to remember

Apply the restriction first, write down the tiles that remain, and divide by a factorial for every letter that still repeats.

Diploma note: permutations with repeated letters and a restriction are a regular Math 30-1 multiple-choice item, and the usual slips are the two above: dividing by the wrong factorials, or multiplying by 2 for the letter that was placed. Writing the remaining letters out before writing the formula avoids both.
