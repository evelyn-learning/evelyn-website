Physics 30 · Item 234035 · Multiple choice · Conservation of momentum

Answer: A. 8.3 m/s, 42° South of East.

Press play. The board draws as the tutor speaks.

  1. ▼

    1. Use conservation of momentum

    In any collision the total momentum just before equals the total momentum just after:

    pbefore = pafterconservation of momentum; it holds whether or not the cars stick

    Momentum is a vector. Car A moves East and car B moves South, so their momenta point in different directions and cannot be added as plain numbers.

    Why not just add 5600 + 5000?

    Adding the numbers assumes both pushes point the same way. They are at right angles, so neither push adds anything in the other's direction. The combined push is the diagonal of the two, which is always less than the plain sum: here 7507, not 10 600.

  2. ▼

    2. Find each car's momentum

    pA = (400 kg)(14 m/s) = 5600 kg·m/s Eastmomentum = mass × velocity; multiply 400 by 14 pB = (500 kg)(10 m/s) = 5000 kg·m/s Southmomentum = mass × velocity; multiply 500 by 10

    Tap an equation line to see the operation behind it.

  3. ▼

    3. Add the two momenta as vectors

    East and South are at right angles, so the two momenta are the legs of a right triangle. The total momentum is the diagonal.

    p2 = pA2 + pB2Pythagorean theorem for a right triangle p2 = (5600)2 + (5000)2substitute the two momenta p2 = 31 360 000 + 25 000 000 = 56 360 000square each number, then add p = √56 360 000 ≈ 7507 kg·m/stake the square root of both sides
    Need a reminder of the Pythagorean theorem?

    In a right triangle the square of the longest side equals the sum of the squares of the other two: c2 = a2 + b2. Here the two momenta are a and b, and the total momentum is c.

  4. ▼

    4. Turn momentum back into velocity

    Check yourself first

    What mass is moving after the collision?

    v = 7507 kg·m/s900 kg ≈ 8.3 m/svelocity = momentum ÷ total mass (400 + 500 = 900 kg) tan θ = oppositeadjacent = 50005600 ≈ 0.893measured from East (adjacent) toward South (opposite) θ = tan−1(0.893) ≈ 41.8° ≈ 42°inverse tangent, calculator in degree mode

    The velocity is 8.3 m/s at 42° South of East, which is option A.

Sanity check

The combined cars cannot move faster than the faster car, and the answer (8.3 m/s) is below 14 m/s. The angle should lean toward East because car A carries the larger momentum, and 42° is less than 45°. Both pass.

One common slip

Measuring the angle from the wrong axis. tan−1(5600 ÷ 5000) = 48° is the angle from South, not from East. The question's answers are written "South of East", so the angle starts at East.

The idea to remember

Momentum is conserved as a vector. Right-angle momenta make a right triangle: Pythagoras for the size, inverse tangent for the direction, then divide by the combined mass.
