- Grade 8 is a FIRST teach of every one of its standards; Algebra 1 re-teaches slope, exponent rules, scientific notation, multi-step equations and the Pythagorean theorem as HS review, so the line above is drawn only at the HS-only extension named per strand — never at the G8 skill itself.
- Number & roots — below (assumed): fraction → decimal by division, ordering rationals, signed-number arithmetic — G7 U1–U2. Above (withheld): simplifying radicals (√50 stays an estimate, never 5√2), radical arithmetic and rationalizing, radical equations, real-number properties as justifications, proving √2 irrational, x² = k for non-perfect k — Algebra 1.
- Exponents — below: whole-number exponents in numerical expressions (G6 U7), (-3)² vs -3² (G7 U2). Above: power of a product/quotient, multi-variable monomials, exponential functions and growth/decay, polynomials — Algebra 1. G8 uses numerical bases (2, 3, 10) and at most a single variable base.
- Proportionality & slope — below: unit rates, complex-fraction rates, the proportional test and the constant of proportionality, all percent applications — G7 U3–U4. Above: zero/undefined slope classification and horizontal/vertical lines as a topic, point-slope and standard form, parallel/perpendicular slope relationships, proportions by cross-multiplication, writing a line from a slope and a point or graphing straight from an equation — Algebra 1 / Geometry.
- Equations & inequalities — below: one- and two-step equations, writing equations from words, one/two-step inequalities, expression simplification — G7 U5–U6. Above: literal equations; ALL inequalities (one-variable, compound, absolute-value) — Algebra 1. No CCSS Grade 8 inequality standard exists.
- Systems — below: nothing (no G7 antecedent). Above: general substitution (isolating a variable first), elimination, method choice, systems of inequalities, graphing from standard form via intercepts, formal classification vocabulary — Algebra 1.
- Functions — below: dependent/independent variables (G6 U8). Above: f(x) notation, domain/range, the named vertical line test, average rate of change over an interval, quadratic and exponential families as named objects, sequences — Algebra 1.
- Transformations & similarity — below: reflecting single points across the axes (G6 U6), scale drawings and area scaling by k² (G7 U7). Above: translation vectors, reflections across arbitrary lines, rotations about centers other than the origin, compositions and symmetry, congruence correspondence and criteria (SSS/SAS/ASA, CPCTC), dilations about other centers, similar-polygon side tests and perimeter/area ratios, SSS/SAS similarity and proportionality theorems — Geometry. (AA similarity as an angle fact IS G8.)
- Angles & triangles — below: complementary/supplementary/vertical angles, triangle inequality and unique-triangle conditions, cross-sections — G7 U7. Above: proving lines parallel, theorem-level justification and two-column proofs, isosceles/equilateral/midsegment/bisector results, polygon angle sums and quadrilateral properties, circle theorems — Geometry.
- Pythagorean theorem — below: nothing (no G7 antecedent). Above: similar-triangle proof, acute/obtuse classification, special right triangles, trigonometric ratios and solving right triangles, the named distance formula and midpoint, circle equations — Geometry.
- Measurement — below: circle circumference/area, prism volume V = Bh, surface area of prisms and pyramids, polygon area — G7 U8. Above: surface area of cylinders/cones/spheres, oblique solids, Cavalieri, hemispheres and composite curved solids, cone slant height, arc length and sector area, regular-polygon area — Geometry.
- Statistics & probability — below: measures of center/variability, sampling and inference, comparing two populations — G7 U9; all probability — G7 U10 (no CCSS Grade 8 probability standard). Above: one-variable statistics with IQR/box plots, correlation strength, correlation vs causation, least-squares/technology-fitted lines, residuals and the correlation coefficient — Algebra 1; conditional probability and independence in two-way tables — no shipped HS seed, still withheld.
