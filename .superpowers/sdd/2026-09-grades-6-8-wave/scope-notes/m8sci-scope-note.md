**Quantitative ceiling (this course):** qualitative-to-light-quantitative only. Speed = distance ÷ time and F = m × a with small whole numbers; "kinetic energy grows with mass, and with the SQUARE of speed" as a described relationship; wave speed = frequency × wavelength as a relationship, never a computation with scientific notation. No vectors, no trigonometry, no algebraic derivations, no joules-by-formula (½mv², mgh), no q = mcΔT, no Coulomb's law, no F = Gm₁m₂/r². Every quantity carries a unit in words; a chemical formula is read out ("H two O") and never written as an arrow equation with coefficients.

**Grade 6 Earth & Space territory (referenced as examples only, never re-taught):**
- Mantle convection and plate motion as an Earth-systems process — the mantle is at most one example of convection; no plates, boundaries or landforms.
- The water cycle — evaporation and condensation are taught as changes of state of a pure substance; the cycle is cited once as an example.
- Weather, air masses, fronts, severe weather — not entered; "warm air rises" is the particle-level fact only.
- Ocean currents and climate — a current is at most one example of convection.
- The carbon cycle at the rock/ocean/air scale and the evidence for rising global temperature — no item uses "greenhouse", the CO₂ record, or fossil-fuel use as content.
- Gravity's role in orbits and the solar system — gravity's dependence on mass and distance and weight-versus-mass only; never back to orbits or planets.
- Minerals identified by hardness, streak, luster, cleavage — this course's characteristic properties are melting point, boiling point, density, solubility, conductivity, flammability.

**Grade 7 Life Science territory (referenced as examples only, never re-taught):**
- Photosynthesis, cellular respiration, and matter/energy through an organism — "energy is released, not made" and "atoms are rearranged, never destroyed" are cited as rules the student already holds, then generalized to non-living systems; no glucose, oxygen-in-a-cell, or organism as a worked example.
- Diffusion and osmosis across a membrane — the general particle model only; no cells.
- Cycles of matter through ecosystems — not entered.
- Scientific-investigation vocabulary (testable question, hypothesis, independent/dependent/controlled variables, fair test, correlation vs causation) — assumed, not re-taught.
- Everything else in Grade 7 (cells, body systems, reproduction, genetics, evolution, classification, ecology, human impact) — never entered.

**HS Chemistry territory (the upward line):**
- Classification of matter by homogeneous/heterogeneous, fixed ratio and separation method — this course stops at pure substance / mixture / element / compound.
- Density as a conversion factor, dimensional analysis, significant figures.
- Subatomic detail: isotopes, mass number, average atomic mass, electron shells and configurations, the history of atomic models, ion formation — this course stops at protons/neutrons/electrons, atomic number, and a neutral atom's equal proton and electron counts.
- Periodic-table organization by valence electrons, periodic trends — this course stops at position, families and observed behavior.
- Bonding types, Lewis structures, VSEPR, polarity, naming and formula writing — this course stops at reading a given simple formula aloud with its atom count.
- Symbolic equations, balancing with coefficients, reaction types, activity series, redox — this course counts atoms from a word-and-picture description only.
- The mole, molar mass, stoichiometry, limiting reactant, percent composition — never entered.
- Gas laws, ideal gas law, kinetic-molecular theory with absolute temperature and the kelvin scale, heating-curve arithmetic, vapor pressure — the particle model, changes of state and temperature stay qualitative.
- Solutions, molarity, solubility curves, colligative properties — dissolving appears only as a physical change.
- ΔH-signed endothermic/exothermic, bond energies, calorimetry (q = mcΔT), specific heat as a number, reaction rates and collision theory, equilibrium — this course stays at "gets warmer / gets colder" and "depends on mass and material".
- Acids, bases, pH, titration, nuclear chemistry, mass-to-energy — never entered.
- Polymer chemistry and organic naming — a synthetic material is traced to its resource and reaction only.

**HS Biology territory:** no item in this course concerns living systems; "energy in living things" continues on the life-science track, not here.

**AP Physics territory (the only upward physics in the catalog):**
- Kinematics with a = Δv/t, vectors, free-body diagrams with angled forces — this course stays in one dimension with small whole numbers; distance-time records are read in words ("flat means at rest, steeper means faster") and never as "slope", which is Grade 8 Math.
- Rearranging F = m × a for an unknown mass, any two-dimensional case.
- Momentum, impulse, work and energy as computed quantities (½mv², mgh, W = Fd) — collisions, kinetic energy, potential energy and energy transfer stay descriptive.
- Coulomb's law, electric fields as computed quantities, capacitance — electric force and fields stay qualitative.
- Current electricity, Ohm's law and circuits — excluded entirely; "current" appears only as the electricity supplied to an electromagnet's coil.
- Right-hand rule, force on a moving charge, induction — magnetism stays at poles, strength factors and the field as a region.
- Buoyancy, Archimedes, pressure, Bernoulli — density only.
- Laws of thermodynamics, entropy, heat engines — thermal energy is qualitative transfer only.
- Period, phase, "energy proportional to amplitude squared", intensity, v = fλ with scientific notation or unit conversion — wave measures are read from a description and the speed relationship is stated in words.
- Snell's law, thin-lens equation, ray-diagram construction, interference — refraction and lenses stay qualitative.
- Photon energy, wave-particle duality, atomic transitions — the electromagnetic spectrum is ordered by wavelength only.
