## Progression rationale (feeds Algebra 1 and Geometry)

- **Number system → real numbers**: Unit 1 completes the middle-school number line with irrationals, roots and estimation — `alg1-u1-real-numbers-operations.ts` re-classifies rational vs irrational as review and `alg1-u9-simplifying-radicals.ts` picks up exactly where 1.4 stops (√50 stays an estimate here; it becomes 5√2 there).
- **Exponents → exponential functions**: Unit 2 owns integer-exponent properties and scientific notation with numerical bases; Algebra 1 U6 repeats the rules with variable bases (its own seeds cite 8.EE.A.1/3/4) and then goes where G8 never does — exponential functions and growth/decay models.
- **Proportionality → linear functions**: G7 U3 ends at y = kx with k identified from tables/graphs; Unit 3 turns k into slope, adds the intercept, and derives y = mx + b from similar triangles. Algebra 1 U4 re-teaches slope and slope-intercept form as review and adds point-slope/standard form and parallel/perpendicular lines.
- **Equations → systems → Algebra 1**: G7 U6 ends at two-step equations; Unit 4 takes one-variable linear equations to their end (both sides, distribution, rational coefficients, solution counts); Unit 5 solves systems by graphing and simple substitution. Algebra 1 U2 and U5 repeat those and add literal equations, general substitution, elimination, and systems of inequalities. No inequality standard exists in Grade 8, so G7 U6 hands inequalities straight to Algebra 1 U3.
- **Functions**: Units 6–7 introduce the function concept, linear vs nonlinear, comparison across representations, and linear models from tables/descriptions — all without f(x) notation, which is `alg1-u4-relations-functions.ts`'s opening move. Volume of cylinders/cones/spheres (row 6.4) is the last volume standard before `geom-u10-*`.
- **Geometry**: Unit 8 is the transformational definition of congruence and similarity that Geometry U4 and U6 formalize (vectors, arbitrary centers, congruence criteria, similarity criteria); Unit 9 gives the informal parallel-line, triangle-angle and Pythagorean arguments that Geometry U3, U5 and U7 turn into theorems and proofs.
- **Statistics**: G7 U9 ends at comparing two populations; Unit 10 moves to two variables — scatter plots, by-eye trend lines, and two-way tables. `alg1-u10-scatterplots-trend-lines.ts` adds strength, correlation vs causation, and technology-fitted lines.
