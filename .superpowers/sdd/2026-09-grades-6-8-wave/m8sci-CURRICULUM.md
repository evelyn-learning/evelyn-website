**Scope ruling (Praveen, 2026-09-02):** the three middle-school science courses follow the NGSS domain rotation — Grade 6 Earth & Space Science (shipped), Grade 7 Life Science (shipped), **Grade 8 Physical Science** (this course). This course stays inside MS-PS1/PS2/PS3/PS4 throughout. The two drift risks this table is built to avoid are HORIZONTAL (re-teaching an Earth-systems or life-science phenomenon that Grade 6 or Grade 7 already owns, instead of supplying the physical MECHANISM underneath it) and UPWARD (reaching into HS Chemistry's quantitative and electron-level content, or into AP-Physics-level mathematics).

**Quantitative ceiling (this course):** qualitative-to-light-quantitative only. Speed = distance ÷ time and F = m × a with small whole numbers; "kinetic energy grows with mass, and with the SQUARE of speed" as a described relationship; wave speed = frequency × wavelength as a relationship, never a computation with scientific notation. No vectors, no trigonometry, no algebraic derivations, no joules-by-formula (½mv², mgh), no q = mcΔT, no Coulomb's law, no F = Gm₁m₂/r².

## ⚠️ For sign-off

1. **Unit order: motion → forces → energy → thermal → matter → atoms → reactions → waves.** Forces-first (Units 1-5) then matter (Units 6-8) then waves (9-10), so that "thermal energy" (Unit 5) is taught before the particle model uses it to explain states of matter (Unit 6). Many textbooks run matter first; the order is a judgement call.
2. **Unit 1 is four DCI-only rows (PS2.A) with no performance expectation of its own.** Reference points, speed = distance ÷ time, distance-time graphs, and velocity/acceleration are the descriptive-motion prerequisites MS-PS2-2 assumes ("change in an object's motion"). NGSS has no MS PE for kinematics; the rows are cited to DCI PS2.A, whose text names reference frames explicitly. Row 1.2 (distance-time graphs) reads the same object as CCSS 8.EE.B.5's "unit rate as slope" — RESOLVED by the controller 2026-09-03: `m8math-CURRICULUM.md` rows 3.1 and 3.3 own slope; this row stays in words ("steeper means faster") and never names slope or writes an equation.
3. **Subatomic structure (row 7.2) exceeds the strict MS-PS1-1 boundary.** NGSS MS PS1.A stops at "substances are made from different types of atoms, which combine"; protons/neutrons/electrons and "atomic number = proton count" are HS-PS1-1 territory but are near-universal Grade 8 state content, and the G6 table explicitly deferred "atomic structure" to this course. Included at the nucleus-plus-electrons level only; isotopes, mass-number arithmetic and electron configurations stay with `chem-u2-*`.
4. **The electromagnetic spectrum (row 10.3) is likewise a light stretch.** MS-PS4-2's assessment boundary limits it to "qualitative applications pertaining to light and mechanical waves"; the spectrum ordered by wavelength (radio → gamma, visible in the middle) is included as the one-lesson answer to "is light a wave like sound?" (DCI PS4.B). No photon energy, no wavelength arithmetic.
5. **Gravity stays qualitative (row 3.1) — the G6 reservation "physics of gravity (F = Gm₁m₂/r²)" is DECLINED.** MS-PS2-4 is "attractive and depends on the masses"; the inverse-square law is HS-PS2-4. The row teaches mass-dependence, distance-dependence in words, and weight vs mass.
6. **Current electricity and circuits are EXCLUDED.** Legacy `g8-sci-electricity.ts` teaches V = IR and Ohm's-law numerics; no MS-PS PE covers circuits (MS-PS2-3 is about electric and magnetic FORCES). The catalog's only owner is `ap-physics2-circuits.ts`, so a Grade 8 student will not meet circuits anywhere in the MS→HS chain of this catalog. Flagging in case the owner wants a circuits row anyway (it would displace one of rows 3.2-3.4).
7. **Merged standards:** MS-PS1-5 (atoms conserved) and the "reactants → products" model are ONE lesson (row 8.2) because Grade 7 already traced atom conservation through an organism (`m7sci-u4-matter-and-energy-in-organisms.ts`, read in full); MS-PS4-1 is split across rows 9.2 (naming wave measures) and 9.4 (amplitude ↔ energy); MS-PS2-1 is split across rows 2.3 (the third law) and 2.4 (collision-safety design); MS-PS2-2 is split across rows 2.1 and 2.2 (first and second law); MS-PS2-3 is split by force type across rows 3.2 (electric) and 3.3 (magnetic); MS-PS1-4 is split across rows 6.2 (change of state) and 6.3 (expansion/density); MS-PS4-2 is split across rows 10.1 (reflect/absorb/transmit) and 10.2 (refraction).
8. **Engineering-design PEs (MS-PS1-6, MS-PS3-3, MS-PS2-1) are assessed as "evaluate a proposed design" MCQs**, not as a build project — the format has no other option. Rows 2.4, 5.4, 8.3 say so.
9. **MS-ETS1 (engineering design process) is not given a row.** It is not an MS-PS standard; the design PEs above carry the engineering practice implicitly. `g8-sci-engineering-design.ts` is therefore not salvaged.
10. **Density (row 6.3) is included; buoyancy/Archimedes is not.** Density is the mechanism G6 withheld ("why mantle material or air moves"); buoyant force is not an MS-PS idea and is owned upward by `ap-physics2-fluids.ts`.

## Progression rationale (NGSS domain rotation: Grade 6 → Grade 7 → Grade 8)

This course is the third and last of the three MS science domains, so it does not deepen Grade 7 the way M8 Math deepens G7 Math; it sits alongside the other two and supplies the physical MECHANISM underneath phenomena those courses described. Per strand:

- **Forces & motion (Units 1-3, MS-PS2)**: entirely new at the MS band — neither shipped science course has a force lesson. The only backward touch is Grade 6's `gravity-and-orbital-motion` (MS-ESS1-2, qualitative "gravity keeps things in orbit"); row 3.1 supplies "gravity depends on mass and distance" underneath it without returning to orbits. Grade 7's fair-test vocabulary (`m7sci-u1-variables-and-controls.ts`) is assumed by the "plan an investigation" PEs (rows 2.2, 3.3, 5.3) and is not re-taught. Upward, every quantitative continuation — vectors, free-body diagrams with angled forces, momentum, Coulomb's law, circuits — is AP Physics; there is no HS physics course in this catalog, so `Explicitly excluded` names the `ap-physics*` seed for each.
- **Energy (Units 4-5, MS-PS3)**: this is where the "energy is transferred and transformed, never created" rule the student met for organisms in Grade 7 Unit 4 becomes a general physical law, and where the heat-transfer mechanisms Grade 6 deliberately withheld from its mantle-convection, water-cycle, weather and ocean-current lessons are taught as content. Rows 4.3, 5.2 and 6.3 each name the G6/G7 file whose phenomenon they now explain, and reference it as an example only. Upward, calorimetry (q = mcΔT) and thermochemistry are `chem-u9-*`.
- **Matter (Units 6-8, MS-PS1)**: Grade 6's "matter cycles, nothing created or destroyed" ran at the rock/water/carbon scale and Grade 7's at the organism scale; this course runs it at the atomic scale (rows 8.2) after building the particle model (Unit 6) and the atom/element/compound vocabulary (Unit 7). The whole strand stops where HS Chemistry's Unit 1-6 begin: no moles, no stoichiometry, no balancing with coefficients, no electron configuration, no bonding types, no equilibrium — each withheld piece names its `chem-*` file in the row's Scope cell.
- **Waves (Units 9-10, MS-PS4)**: entirely new at the MS band and terminal within it; the only backward touch is Grade 6's radiation-based evidence for warming (row 10.3 cites it, never re-teaches it). Upward continuation is AP Physics 2 optics/modern.
- **Scientific-investigation skills**: as in Grade 6, this course has no dedicated inquiry unit; Grade 7 Unit 1 (`m7sci-u1-*`, four files) formally owns testable questions, variables, controls, and data-reading. The "plan an investigation" and "argue from evidence" PEs here (2.2, 3.3, 3.4, 4.4, 5.3, 9.4) exercise those skills inside physical-science content without re-teaching the vocabulary.
