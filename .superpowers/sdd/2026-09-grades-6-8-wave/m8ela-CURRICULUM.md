## ⚠️ For sign-off

1. **Row 4.4 (RI.8.7, choosing the medium for an idea) is the one row that is not read from an excerpt.** The course has no media; the lesson teaches the *reasoning* about which medium (paragraph, table or diagram, audio, video) serves a stated purpose, and each item describes the candidate presentations in words. It is the same code family as G7's `m7ela-u3-text-features-and-graphics.ts` (RI.7.5 + RI.7.7). If the owner prefers a pure-text row, the drop-in replacement is an RI.8.1 row, "Strongest Evidence in an Informational Text", parallel to row 1.1.
2. **Verbals (L.8.1a) are split into two lessons** — gerunds & participles (the -ing lookalike pair) and infinitives & verbal phrases — not one and not three. One lesson cannot install three functions in 20 minutes; three would leave the infinitive lesson thin.
3. **L.8.1d (shifts in voice and mood) is its own row (6.1)**, separate from the U5 identification rows, on the same lineage as G6's L.6.1c number/person consistency row and G7's tense-consistency row (`m7ela-u5-verb-tense-consistency.ts`). Both of those are whole-passage repair rows, and this one is too.
4. **L.8.3a (voice and mood for effect) is a separate row (6.2)** from the U5 rows that teach what active/passive and the five moods *are*. Identification and deliberate choice are different lessons; G7 made the same split between naming sentence types and combining for emphasis.
5. **Unit 9 is 2 informative + 2 narrative** (G6 and G7 were 3 + 1). W.8.3 adds two genuinely new narrative moves — reflection (W.8.3b) and time/setting shifts (W.8.3c) — that G7's `m7ela-u9-narrative-technique.ts` does not touch. The cost: W.8.2c (varied transitions) gets no row, because G7 U9.3 teaches transition families in full and "varied" alone is half a lesson; W.8.2e (formal style) is taught once, in row 8.4, and applies to both modes.
6. **Objective summary is NOT re-taught** anywhere in G8, although RL.8.2 and RI.8.2 both name it. G7 teaches it end to end in `m7ela-u2-theme-and-summary.ts` and `m7ela-u3-summarizing-informational-text.ts`; G8 rows 2.1 and 3.1 take the *development* half of those codes only.
7. **Allusions (row 2.3) depend on cultural reference knowledge** a thirteen-year-old may not have. The rule for bank items: every allusion item embeds a one-line gloss of the reference in the excerpt or stem, so the item tests the *effect* of the allusion, never recall of the reference. Confirm this is acceptable rather than dropping allusion and keeping only analogy.
8. **RL.8.9 (row 2.4, modern stories drawing on traditional patterns)** is taught from short original excerpts plus a stated pattern (a hero's three trials, a trickster who is outwitted, a warning ignored). Traditional-story *patterns* are subject matter, not quoted text, so no public-domain or copyrighted passage is ever reproduced. Confirm the owner is comfortable with pattern-recognition at excerpt scale as the whole of RL.8.9.
9. **Ellipsis for omission (L.8.2b) is merged with ellipsis for a pause (L.8.2a) into row 6.4.** Each alone is half a lesson; together they are one, and the pair is exactly the two jobs the mark does.
10. **Declined outright, each with its reason in "Explicitly excluded":** L.8.2c (spelling), RL.8.7 (film or live production vs. text), W.8.8's plagiarism-avoidance half (G7 owns it), W.8.1e / W.8.2f conclusions (G6 and G7 own them), and all of SL.8.
11. **Rule 7 is vacuous for ELA.** `m6ela-CURRICULUM.md`'s "Explicitly excluded (reserved for G7/G8)" section names only G7 rows, and no `m6ela-*.ts` seed mentions Grade 8 (`grep -i "grade 8\|G8\|grade-8" m6ela-*.ts` → no matches). There is nothing G6 reserved for G8 to pick up.

## Progression rationale (from G7, toward HS English)

- **Reading literature — evidence, character, structure (Unit 1)**: G7 U1 cites evidence and maps plot stages, conflict type, characterization moves, and setting's effect (`m7ela-u1-*`). G8 escalates each: evidence is RANKED for strength (RL.8.1); dialogue and incidents are analyzed for what they DO to the plot and the character (RL.8.3, split by pedagogical stage); and structure is compared across TWO texts (RL.8.5), which no G6 or G7 row attempts. HS `engl-u6-*` and `engl-u8-poetic-form-and-structure.ts` then add motive inference, flashback/foreshadowing as structural choices, and fixed poetic forms.
- **Reading literature — theme, irony, allusion, patterns (Unit 2)**: G7 U2 states a theme, traces its development, writes an objective summary, sorts narrators, and names five figures plus tone and mood. G8 takes the parts RL.8.2/8.4/8.6/8.9 add: the theme's relationship to character and setting; dramatic irony as a reader-vs-character gap; allusion and analogy as imported meaning; and traditional patterns rendered new. Objective summary, figure identification, and tone-vs-mood are not re-taught. HS `engl-u6-theme.ts`, `engl-u6-narrative-point-of-view.ts`, and `engl-u8-imagery-and-symbolism.ts` then add motifs, the unreliable narrator, and symbolism.
- **Reading informational texts — development, connections, paragraph structure (Unit 3)**: G7 U3 identifies a central idea, summarizes, reads text features, and determines technical meaning. G8 moves to how the central idea DEVELOPS and relates to supporting ideas (RI.8.2), how the text CONNECTS and DISTINGUISHES ideas (RI.8.3, new), word choice's impact on TONE in nonfiction (RI.8.4), and the role of a sentence inside one PARAGRAPH (RI.8.5, scale dropped from the whole text). HS `engl-u7-*` re-runs these at 9-10 register with inference-plus-background-knowledge and "why this structure" analysis.
- **Reading informational texts — author's response, reasoning, conflicting texts, medium (Unit 4)**: G7 U4 names purpose and perspective, judges evidence relevance and sufficiency, and compares two accurate texts by emphasis and interpretation. G8 asks how the author ANSWERS the other side (RI.8.6), whether the REASONING is sound (RI.8.8, fallacies unnamed), where two texts CONFLICT on fact vs. interpretation (RI.8.9), and which MEDIUM should carry an idea (RI.8.7). HS `engl-u5-logical-fallacies.ts`, `engl-u5-rhetorical-appeals.ts`, and `engl-u10-evaluating-sources.ts` then name the fallacies, the appeals, and lateral reading.
- **Grammar (Unit 5)**: G7 U5 + U6.1 teach parts of speech, agreement, pronoun-antecedent repair, tense consistency, and phrase vs. clause via the subject-verb test — including the fact that an -ing word or a to-form is "not acting as the verb". G8 names what those forms ARE (verbals, L.8.1a, two lessons) and adds active/passive voice (L.8.1b) and the five verb moods (L.8.1c), none of which any G7 or G6 row touches (grep verified). HS `engl-u1-*` and `engl-u2-modifiers-and-parallelism.ts` then add perfect tenses, dangling modifiers, and parallelism; no HS seed teaches mood, so G8 owns it outright.
- **Sentence style & punctuation (Unit 6)**: G7 U6 teaches sentence types, combining for emphasis, fragments/run-ons, and the five comma jobs plus apostrophes. G8 adds the whole-passage voice/mood repair row (L.8.1d) on the G6 pronoun-consistency and G7 tense-consistency lineage, deliberate voice/mood choice for effect (L.8.3a), the single dash and pause comma (L.8.2a), and the ellipsis for pause and omission (L.8.2a/b). HS `engl-u3-*` then owns matched dash/parenthesis pairs, quotation punctuation, semicolons, and colons.
- **Vocabulary (Unit 7)**: G7 U7 teaches four context-clue types, roots/prefixes/suffixes with the four-step routine, polarity connotation, and confusable pairs. G8 escalates each in its own code family: multi-sense dictionary disambiguation by part of speech (L.8.4c/d), root FAMILIES differing by prefix (L.8.4b), verbal irony and puns (L.8.5a, new), and shades of meaning by degree and formality within one polarity (L.8.5c). HS `engl-u4-*` then adds precision/concision and register for a named audience.
- **Argument writing (Unit 8)**: G7 U8 teaches the debatable claim, evidence types plus elaboration, the counterclaim/rebuttal two-move (reasoning only, by its own header), and paragraph order plus conclusion. G8 makes the claim DISTINGUISH itself from the opposing claim (W.8.1a), brings EVIDENCE from credible sources to the rebuttal (W.8.1b), builds clause-level COHESION among claim, counterclaim, and evidence (W.8.1c), and installs FORMAL STYLE (W.8.1d, no G7 row). HS `engl-u9-thesis-statements.ts` and `engl-u5-counterargument-and-rebuttal.ts` then add the previewing thesis and the hidden-assumption rebuttal.
