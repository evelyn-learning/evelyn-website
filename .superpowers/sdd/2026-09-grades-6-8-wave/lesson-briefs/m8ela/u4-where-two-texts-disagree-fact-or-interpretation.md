## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given two short original texts on the same topic that CONFLICT, identify exactly where they disagree and classify the disagreement as a matter of FACT (a number, a date, what happened — checkable) or of INTERPRETATION (the same fact read two ways), and say what a reader should do next in each case. Builds on `m7ela-u4-comparing-two-texts.ts` (RI.7.9: emphasis vs. interpretation; "different does not mean dishonest"), which compares two accurate texts; RI.8.9 introduces the case where at least one text is wrong on a fact.
