## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Evaluate whether the REASONING in a short argument is sound — whether the reason actually leads to the claim, and whether the evidence proves what is claimed rather than something nearby (a bigger claim, a different group, a different time) — and recognize evidence introduced to distract from a weak link. Builds on `m7ela-u4-tracing-an-argument.ts` (RI.7.8: is each piece of evidence relevant and is there enough of it), which judges evidence and never the logical link; G8 judges the link. Fallacies are NOT named here. Stops short of HS `engl-u5-logical-fallacies.ts` (RI.9-10.8: ad hominem, straw man, false dilemma, bandwagon, hasty generalization, slippery slope, false cause).
