## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Use a single dash for an abrupt break or a sharp emphasis after a complete thought, and a comma for a softer pause, and choose between them by the effect wanted — punctuation for a PAUSE or BREAK, which is the new L.8.2a job. Builds on `m7ela-u6-commas-and-end-punctuation.ts` (L.7.2: the five comma jobs — series, introductory element, joining two sentences, extra information with the removal test, coordinate adjectives — plus apostrophes) and G6's `m6ela-u6-commas-for-nonrestrictive-and-parenthetical-elements.ts` (L.6.2a: a PAIR of commas, parentheses, or dashes to set off extra information). G8 does not re-teach any of those jobs; it adds the single dash for a break and the pause comma. Stops short of HS `engl-u3-dashes-parentheses-quotation.ts` (L.9-10.2b: matched pairs never mixed; quotation-mark punctuation) and `engl-u3-semicolons-and-colons.ts` (L.9-10.2a).
