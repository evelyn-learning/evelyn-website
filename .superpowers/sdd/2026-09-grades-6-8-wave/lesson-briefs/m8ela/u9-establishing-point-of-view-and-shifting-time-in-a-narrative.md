## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Choose and hold a narrative point of view from the opening sentence (first person or third person limited, matching what the story needs the reader to know), and use transition words, phrases, and clauses to signal a shift in TIME or SETTING (a jump forward, a look back, a change of place) so the reader is never lost. Builds on `m7ela-u9-narrative-technique.ts` (W.7.3b/d: dialogue that does a job, pacing, sensory description, precise words) and on the reading-side `m7ela-u2-point-of-view.ts` (RL.7.6); G7's narrative row never chooses a point of view or moves the clock. G6's `m6ela-u9-orienting-the-reader-in-a-narrative.ts` (W.6.3a) establishes context and characters, not a deliberate POV choice.
