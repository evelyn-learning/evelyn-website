## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described two-country, two-good table of what each can produce with the same effort, show why both gain when each specializes in what it produces relatively better and trades, then evaluate a described multi-country supply chain for its single points of failure (one supplier, one strait, one port) and the cost of a backup. Deepens G7 5.4, which taught why places trade, import/export from either side, and tracing a supply chain; G8 quantifies the gain and evaluates the chain's fragility.
