## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described multi-decade series (annual temperature departures, sea level, ice extent, frost-free days), distinguish a long-run trend from year-to-year variability, explain why one cold year does not reverse a warming trend and why climate is defined over decades, and describe from described regional data how impacts fall unevenly (coasts, dry regions, the Arctic). Deepens G7 2.3, which distinguished weather from climate and taught the five controls; G8 reads change over time in the data. Because no MS science course owns the greenhouse mechanism (see For sign-off #7, RULED 2026-09-03: absorb), this row ALSO carries a one-paragraph mechanism at define depth — sunlight warms the surface, the surface gives off heat, certain gases in the air (carbon dioxide, water vapor, methane) hold some of that heat in, and more of those gases hold more heat — with no radiative-forcing arithmetic; no attribution debate is taught.
