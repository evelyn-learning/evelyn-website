## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given described location factors for several candidate sites (labor cost, transport to market, port access, nearby suppliers, reliable power, land cost), predict where a described firm locates and why, explain clustering of similar firms, and evaluate the described effects on the place gained and the place left. Deepens G7 5.1 `m7geo-u5-economic-systems.ts` (economic activity is shaped by the resources, climate, land and location a place has) and G7 10.3 `m7geo-u10-asia-population-and-economy.ts` (coastal position and deep harbors allow export manufacturing); G8 makes the siting decision multi-factor and two-sided.
