## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described coastal area with elevation bands, population per band and a stated rise or storm-surge height, identify which described zones flood, estimate how many people are exposed, and evaluate the three response families — protect (walls, dunes), accommodate (raised buildings, zoning), retreat (relocation) — by cost, permanence and who is affected. Deepens G6 10.3 (physical geography changes over time, e.g. a coastline eroding) and G7 2.1 (coastal landforms); G8 quantifies exposure and evaluates responses. Not yet coastal-process physics or insurance economics.
