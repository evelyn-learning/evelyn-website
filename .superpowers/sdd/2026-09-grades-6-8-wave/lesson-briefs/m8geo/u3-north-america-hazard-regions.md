## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Explain why each major hazard clusters in a particular part of North America — earthquakes and volcanoes on the Pacific margin, hurricanes on the Atlantic and Gulf coasts, tornadoes where warm Gulf air meets cold dry air over the Plains, wildfire in the dry West, blizzards and permafrost in the North — and, given a described event with its location and the number of people and buildings exposed, classify it as a hazard or a disaster and say what would change the classification. Deepens G7 2.2, which taught that earthquake and volcano zones cluster along plate boundaries and the hazard-versus-disaster distinction; G8 adds atmospheric hazards, maps them to regions, and applies the distinction to exposure data. Risk quantification and response evaluation are withheld for Unit 7; the physics of how a hurricane or tornado forms is Grade 6 Science (`m6sci` 6.3).
