## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given two described maps of the same described data that differ in projection, symbol sizing, color ordering, title wording or what they leave out, identify which design choice produces a different impression and evaluate whether a stated claim is supported by the data or only by the design. Deepens G7 1.1 `m7geo-u1-maps-globes-and-projections.ts`, which taught that every flat map distorts shape, area, distance or direction and how to choose a projection for a question; G8 turns choice into critique. Not yet propaganda-map history or GIS symbology procedures.
