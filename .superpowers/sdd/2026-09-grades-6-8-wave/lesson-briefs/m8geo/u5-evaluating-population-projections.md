## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described growth rate, estimate doubling time with the rule of 70, compare two described projections built on different assumptions (constant versus falling birth rate; with versus without net migration), and explain why a projection is a conditional statement rather than a prediction and which assumption a stated headline depends on. Deepens G7 3.2, which distinguished the size of a population from the rate at which it grows; G8 turns the rate into a time horizon and evaluates the assumptions.
