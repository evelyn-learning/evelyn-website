## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described invented or long-settled case (a river that shifted channel, a straight-line boundary drawn through a valley, a resource straddling the line, two differing historical accounts), identify which of the predictable causes the evidence supports, classify the boundary type, and evaluate which resolution mechanism fits (joint survey commission, treaty, arbitration, court). Deepens G7 6.3, which classified boundaries as natural, geometric or cultural, taught territory and sovereignty, and listed the general causes of disputes and the ways states cooperate; G8 applies the framework to case evidence.
