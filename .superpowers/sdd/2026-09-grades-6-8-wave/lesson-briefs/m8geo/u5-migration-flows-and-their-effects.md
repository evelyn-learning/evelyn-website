## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described flow table between several invented countries with their described wage levels, job openings and existing migrant communities, identify net senders and receivers, explain the pattern using push and pull plus two ideas new at this depth — intervening obstacles (cost, distance, borders) and chain migration — and evaluate the described effects on both origin (money sent home, loss of trained workers) and destination (labor supply, pressure on housing) without characterizing any group. Deepens G7 3.3, which taught push/pull and the voluntary/forced and internal/international distinctions; G8 adds obstacles and chains and evaluates two-sided effects from data. Not yet migration policy or any live political debate.
