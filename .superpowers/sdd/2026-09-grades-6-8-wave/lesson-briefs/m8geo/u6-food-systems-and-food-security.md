## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given described data on two farming systems (small mixed farms feeding a local area versus large single-crop farms selling to distant markets: yield per hectare, inputs, water use, labor), compare them on output and on the trade-offs they carry (irrigation and salt build-up, fertilizer runoff, dependence on one crop's price), and evaluate a described region's food security by availability, access and stability rather than by total output alone. Deepens G7 7.4 (why land is cleared, terraced, irrigated; who gains and who bears the cost) and G7 5.2; G8 compares systems on data and evaluates security. Named agricultural land-use models and the formal agricultural-systems typology are withheld — AP Human Geography `ap-human-geo-agriculture.ts`.
