## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described origin-destination table of moves between North American regions and a described set of conditions in each region, identify net sending and net receiving regions, sort the stated reasons into push and pull, and evaluate which explanation the data supports for the long-running interior-to-South-and-West shift and for rural-to-metropolitan movement; treat immigration to the United States and Canada as flows read from described data, never as a story. Deepens G7 3.3 `m7geo-u3-migration-push-and-pull.ts`, which taught push and pull, voluntary versus forced, internal versus international; G8 applies the sorting to a flow table and evaluates explanations.
