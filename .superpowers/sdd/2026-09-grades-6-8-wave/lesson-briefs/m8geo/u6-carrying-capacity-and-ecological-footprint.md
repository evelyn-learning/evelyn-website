## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given described per-person footprint and available productive land for several invented places, compute whether each is within or beyond its own capacity, explain why carrying capacity is not fixed (technology, trade, and imports move it), and evaluate a stated overshoot claim for what it counts and what it leaves out.
