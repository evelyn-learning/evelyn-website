## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described town with parcels, a flood zone, a road network and three stakeholder groups, evaluate two described zoning plans using buffer and overlay reasoning (Unit 2), hazard exposure (Unit 7) and urban form (Unit 4), and state which plan better meets a stated criterion and which group bears its cost. Deepens G6 10.4 `m6geo-u10-using-geography-to-plan-a-community.ts` (how a planner uses land, water and resource facts to place roads, parks, farms and housing) and G7 3.4 (what rapid growth puts pressure on); G8 compares competing plans on data and trade-offs.
