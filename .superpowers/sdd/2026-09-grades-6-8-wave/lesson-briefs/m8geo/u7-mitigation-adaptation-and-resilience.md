## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Distinguish mitigation (reducing the hazard's cause or size) from adaptation (reducing harm when it happens), classify described actions correctly, and evaluate a described community resilience plan (early warning, building codes, land-use zoning, relocation help) by cost, by which action helps which residents, and by the trade-off it makes. Deepens G7 7.4, which taught stating a modification's trade-off by naming what is gained and given up and who gains and who bears the cost; G8 applies that framing to hazard policy with data.
