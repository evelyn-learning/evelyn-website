## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given described data on internet and mobile access by place and by income group, identify where access gaps lie (rural versus urban, high- versus low-income households), explain why a newer technology can spread where an older network never reached, and evaluate what improved connectivity changes for schooling, banking and markets in a described place. Deepens G7 9.4 `m7geo-u9-africa-middle-east-development.ts` (a technology such as mobile money can spread without waiting for an older network) and G7 4.4 `m7geo-u4-cultural-change-and-globalization.ts` (globalization spreads traits; the disagreement about it, without taking a side); G8 measures the gap and evaluates its effects.
