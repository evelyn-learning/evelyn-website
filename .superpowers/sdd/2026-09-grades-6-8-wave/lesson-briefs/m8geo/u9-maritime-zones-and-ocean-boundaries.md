## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given a described coastline geometry with distances, apply the standard zones (a 12-nautical-mile territorial sea; a 200-nautical-mile exclusive economic zone in which the coastal state controls fishing and seabed resources; the high seas beyond), decide which described activity falls in which zone, explain why a small island can extend a zone greatly, and reason how two overlapping zones are split by a median line. Deepens G7 6.3 (territory and sovereignty as the state making its own laws inside its area); G8 extends sovereignty offshore as measured geometry. No real contested claim is used.
