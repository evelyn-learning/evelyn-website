## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Given described indicator tables for two invented regions across physical (climate, water, hazards), population (density, age structure, migration), economic (activity mix, export concentration) and connectivity dimensions, compare them on a stated criterion, decide which faces the greater challenge on that criterion and why the answer flips on a different criterion, and explain why a region is a tool geographers make rather than a fact of nature. Synthesizes at analysis depth what G7 Units 7–10 taught region by region (e.g. 9.4 — why a single label never fits a region of dozens of countries) and G7 1.4 `m7geo-u1-regions-and-place.ts` (formal, functional and perceptual regions).
