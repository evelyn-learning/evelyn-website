## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Deepens G7 3.4, which defined urbanization as a share and taught linear, clustered and dispersed patterns, and contrasts G7 7.3 `m7geo-u7-latin-america-economy-and-cities.ts`, which taught the primate city; G8 measures form and evaluates policy.
