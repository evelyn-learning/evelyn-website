## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Describe a wave as a repeating disturbance that carries energy from place to place WITHOUT carrying the matter along (a floating duck bobs but does not travel with the ripple), classify a described wave as transverse (the medium moves across the direction of travel — rope, water surface) or longitudinal (the medium squeezes and stretches along it — sound, a pushed slinky), and state that mechanical waves need a medium (DCI PS4.A; the entry lesson for MS-PS4-1).
