## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Distinguish temperature (a measure of the AVERAGE kinetic energy of a sample's particles) from thermal energy (the TOTAL, which also depends on how much matter there is — a bathtub of warm water holds more thermal energy than a cup of boiling water), using the particle-motion picture (DCI PS3.A, the prerequisite for MS-PS3-4; no PE names the temperature/thermal-energy distinction directly).
