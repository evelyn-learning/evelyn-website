## Scope — teach exactly this, and nothing adjacent

Contrast sound (a mechanical wave that needs a medium and is silent in the vacuum of space) with light (a wave that crosses empty space — sunlight reaches Earth through nothing), and place the members of the electromagnetic spectrum in order of wavelength — radio, microwave, infrared, visible (red to violet), ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma — with one everyday use each (DCI PS4.B "because light can travel through space, it cannot be a matter wave"; the spectrum is a light stretch beyond MS-PS4-2's boundary — sign-off 4).
