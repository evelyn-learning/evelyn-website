### What Is the AI Voice Tutor?

The Evelyn Learning AI Voice Tutor is a multimodal tutoring system that combines natural voice conversation with a synchronized interactive whiteboard. Students speak to an AI tutor as they would a human teacher, and the tutor responds verbally while simultaneously rendering equations, graphs, diagrams, and visualizations on screen.

Unlike text-only AI chatbots, the Voice Tutor replicates the experience of sitting across from a skilled private tutor who can draw on a whiteboard while explaining a concept — at a fraction of the cost, available 24/7.

---

### Core Capabilities

#### 1. Real-Time Voice Conversation
Students interact through natural speech. The AI tutor listens, understands, and responds conversationally — no typing required. Two voice engine tiers are available:

- **Standard Engine** — Turn-by-turn voice pipeline with ~1.5s response time. High-quality speech recognition, AI reasoning, and natural speech synthesis. Cost-effective for large-scale deployments. **$0.06/minute.**
- **Premium Engine** — Ultra-low-latency voice with sub-400ms response time. Seamless turn-taking with natural interruptions, emotional expressiveness, and 8 voice options. Ideal for premium tutoring tiers and competitive exam prep. **$0.25/minute.**
- **Text-only mode** — Students type, tutor responds in text. Full whiteboard support. **$0.02/minute.**

Both voice engines support 50+ languages, natural turn-taking, and multiple voice options.

#### 2. Interactive Whiteboard with 19 Visual Types
The tutor's signature feature. As the AI explains a concept verbally, it simultaneously generates visuals on the whiteboard:

| Category | Visual Types |
|----------|-------------|
| **Mathematics** | LaTeX equations, interactive function graphs (position-time, velocity-time, generic), number lines, fraction bars, matrices, unit circles, geometry (points, segments, polygons, circles, angles) |
| **Graphs & Data** | Interactive function plotting with pan/zoom, data tables, statistical displays |
| **Diagrams** | Free-body diagrams, vector diagrams, projectile motion, circular motion, pipe flow, Venn diagrams, tree diagrams |
| **Science** | 3D molecular structures (chemistry), SVG-based custom diagrams |
| **Programming** | Syntax-highlighted code blocks |

All visuals are generated in real-time, synchronized with the tutor's verbal explanation. The whiteboard supports multi-page navigation, allowing students to refer back to previous work.

#### 3. Socratic Teaching Method
The AI tutor does not simply provide answers. It employs the Socratic method:

- **Diagnose first**: Identifies where the student's understanding breaks down
- **Guide via questions**: Leads students to discover answers themselves
- **Multiple explanation approaches**: Graphical, algebraic, intuitive, analogy-based, experimental
- **Misconception detection**: Recognizes common errors and addresses root causes, not symptoms
- **Adaptive difficulty**: Adjusts language and complexity to the student's demonstrated level

#### 4. Homework Photo Upload
Students can photograph their homework or exam problems. The AI extracts the problem text, understands the mathematical notation, and begins a guided tutoring session around that specific problem — complete with whiteboard visuals.

#### 5. Session Persistence & Analytics
Every tutoring session is recorded with:
- Full transcript (student and tutor exchanges)
- All whiteboard commands and visuals generated
- Token usage and cost attribution
- Session duration, subject, topic, and learning goal
- Debug events for quality assurance

### Pedagogical Approach

The Voice Tutor is designed around evidence-based teaching practices:

1. **Concrete before abstract**: Every new concept begins with a tangible example or visual before introducing formal notation
2. **Active recall over passive review**: The tutor asks questions and poses problems rather than lecturing
3. **Immediate feedback**: Errors are caught and addressed in real-time with explanations
4. **Scaffolded difficulty**: Problems progress from guided to independent as mastery increases
5. **Multi-representational**: The same concept is shown algebraically, graphically, and intuitively
6. **Grade-appropriate language**: K-2 students receive simple language with colorful visuals; AP students receive college-level rigor and precise terminology

### Language Support

- The voice engine supports **50+ languages** for speech input and output, including Arabic, French, English, Spanish, Hindi, Urdu, Turkish, Mandarin, Portuguese, German, and many more
- The whiteboard renders formal mathematical and scientific notation independently of the spoken language
- This enables **code-switching**: verbal instruction in one language while formal notation appears in standard international form on the whiteboard
