# Voice Tutor — Validator Test Prompts

## show_circuit

1. Show a circuit with a 9V battery, a 150Ω resistor, and a 100Ω resistor in parallel, with an ammeter measuring the current through the 150Ω branch
2. Draw a circuit with two batteries in series (6V and 9V) connected to three 100Ω resistors in parallel
3. Show an RC charging circuit: 12V battery, 1 kΩ resistor, and a 100 µF capacitor in series with a switch
4. Draw a circuit with three batteries in series (6V, 9V, 12V) connected to three 100Ω resistors and two 50Ω resistors in parallel
5. Show a series circuit with a 9V battery and three resistors of 10Ω, 20Ω, and 30Ω
6. Draw a circuit with a battery, a switch, two parallel branches each with one 100Ω resistor, and a voltmeter across one branch

## show_collision

1. Show a 1D elastic collision: a 2 kg ball moving at 5 m/s hits a 2 kg ball at rest. Show the before and after.
2. Show a perfectly inelastic collision: a 3 kg cart at 4 m/s collides with a 1 kg cart at rest. They stick together.
3. Draw an inelastic collision: a 5 kg block at 6 m/s rear-ends a 2 kg block at 1 m/s. After the collision the 5 kg block moves at 4 m/s.
4. Show a 2D collision: a 1 kg puck moving at vx = 4 m/s along x hits a 1 kg puck at rest. After, puck A moves at vx=2, vy=2 and puck B moves at vx=2, vy=-2.
5. Show three balls in a row colliding 1D: a 1 kg ball at 5 m/s, a 2 kg ball at rest, and a 3 kg ball at -2 m/s. After: the 1 kg moves at -1 m/s, the 2 kg at 3 m/s, the 3 kg at 0 m/s.
6. Show an elastic collision: a 4 kg object at 3 m/s hits a 4 kg object at -1 m/s. After they stick together at 1 m/s.

## show_energy_bars

1. Show an energy bar chart for a 2 kg ball dropped from 10 m. Show three positions: top, middle at 5 m, and bottom. Use g = 10 m/s².
2. Show an energy bar chart for a 2 kg ball dropped from 10 m onto a spring with k = 500 N/m. Show four positions: at release, just before impact, at maximum compression, and rebound to 6 m. Use g = 10 m/s².
3. A 5 kg block slides down a 10 m ramp with friction. Show the energy bar chart at three positions: top, middle, and bottom. The total energy starts at 200 J of PE; by the bottom, 60 J has been lost to friction.
4. Show the energy bar chart for a pendulum at four positions: leftmost extreme, equilibrium passing right, rightmost extreme, equilibrium passing left. Maximum height gives 50 J of PE.
5. Show an energy bar chart for a ball dropped from a 10 m cliff into a 5 m pit. Use the cliff edge as PE = 0. Top: PE = 100 J, KE = 0. Cliff edge: PE = 0, KE = 100 J. Pit bottom: PE = -50 J, KE = 150 J.
6. Show a roller coaster with friction at four positions: start, mid, low point, end. Thermal energy lost: 0 J at start, 30 J at mid, 20 J at low point, 50 J at end.
