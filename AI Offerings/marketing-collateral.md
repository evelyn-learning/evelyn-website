# FAQ SECTION

**Q: Is the AI going to replace our tutors?**
A: Absolutely not. Our AI handles routine tasks—answering common questions, providing initial feedback, generating practice materials—so your tutors can focus on what humans do best: building relationships, providing motivation, and tackling complex concepts.

**Q: How accurate is the essay scoring?**
A: Our system achieves 95%+ correlation with human graders on standardized essay rubrics. Every score includes detailed reasoning, and tutors can review and adjust as needed.

**Q: Can we customize it with our branding?**
A: Yes. All student-facing components can be white-labeled with your logo, colors, and tone of voice. Your students never see our brand—only yours.

**Q: What about student data privacy?**
A: We're SOC 2 compliant and FERPA-aware. Student conversations are encrypted, never used for general AI training, and can be deleted on request. We can sign BAAs for organizations that require them.

A: Basic integration (Homework Bot, Essay Scorer) can go live in 1-2 weeks. Full platform deployment with LMS integration typically takes 4-6 weeks.
