# ABOUT PAGE

## Hero
**Headline:** We Started as Educators. We Became AI Innovators.
**Subheadline:** For 20 years, we've been obsessed with one question: How do students actually learn? Now we're building AI that knows the answer.

## Our Story

**Opening:**
Evelyn Learning began in 2003 with a simple belief: great educational content is created by great teachers. We built a team of 300+ educators—not just subject matter experts, but actual teachers who understand how to explain concepts in ways that click.

**The Pivot:**
But as AI transformed every industry, we saw an opportunity. All those years of creating educational content taught us what works and what doesn't. We have over a million pieces of content that demonstrate effective pedagogy. We know what questions students ask, what misconceptions they hold, what explanations break through.

**Today:**
Now we're channeling that expertise into AI products. Our models aren't trained on random internet text—they're trained on decades of educational best practices. The result? AI that teaches like our best teachers do.

## Our Approach

**Headline:** AI Trained by Teachers, Not Just Engineers

**Philosophy Points:**
1. **Pedagogy First:** We don't just fine-tune language models. We embed teaching methodology into every product.
2. **Guide, Don't Give:** Our AI helps students discover answers, not memorize them.
3. **Human in the Loop:** AI amplifies educators—it doesn't replace them.
4. **Outcome Focused:** We measure success by student improvement, not just engagement.
